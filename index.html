✉️ My Apology

Dear Tita Shine,

I have spent a lot of time reflecting on what happened, and I realized that no explanation can change the fact that I disappointed you.

I sincerely apologize for my actions. I take full responsibility for what happened, and I am not here to make excuses.

This experience taught me that trust is something that should always be protected.

I learned that good intentions are not enough. Maturity means understanding how my actions affect the people who care about me.

Respect is shown through self-control, responsibility, and knowing boundaries.

🤝 My Promise

  • I will show change through actions, not only words.

🌿 My Commitment

My commitment is not to prove myself through one apology, but through consistency.

I understand rebuilding trust takes time, and I am willing to be patient.
